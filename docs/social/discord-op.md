# ⚽ LMASTER Mod
A fan-made fix-and-restore mod for *Let's Make a Soccer Team!* on PS2 (European release, SLES-54151).

📥 **Download:** <https://github.com/NexxusDrakoMultimedia/LMASTER-Mod/releases/latest>
📖 **Instructions:** <https://github.com/NexxusDrakoMultimedia/LMASTER-Mod#readme>
📜 **Changelog:** <https://github.com/NexxusDrakoMultimedia/LMASTER-Mod/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md>

## Editions
- **Lite:** the bugs fixed and cut features restored. Uses your existing saves.
- **Full:** Lite plus a hidden third tier, fairer fixtures, and the real names and grounds of 2005-06. Original difficulty. Own saves, new career.
- **Max:** Full plus a harder economy and balance changes. Own saves, new career.

## What you need
1. Your own disc image of the game, matching the [Redump dump](<https://redump.info/disc/12334/>) exactly (not .cso or .chd)
2. PCSX2
3. Delta Patcher (Windows) or xdelta3 (Mac/Linux)
4. The mod's zip

Keep a copy of your original .iso: every new version patches it again.

## Good to know
- ⚠️ **This is testing software.** Releases come often, and any one may need a new career. The changelog says so at the top when it does. Delete old careers yourself: the game won't stop you loading them.
- Changes marked *needs extra testing* haven't been tried in enough real games yet. Tell us how they go, good or bad!
- Real logos and textures are the sister project **LMAST-Realism**: <https://github.com/NexxusDrakoMultimedia/LMAST-Realism>
- Report bugs here or on GitHub: <https://github.com/NexxusDrakoMultimedia/LMASTER-Mod/issues>
